## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PaintKing.in is a B2B industrial surface-finishing company (est. 2000, 51-200 employees) with two units — Kandivali West, Mumbai and SIDCUL, Haridwar. Core services: Spray Painting, Hydrographic (Water Transfer) Printing, Digital Printing, and Injection Moulding — serving OEMs in automotive, toys, appliances, and consumer durables.

CURRENT GAPS IDENTIFIED:
- Blog exists but is thin (7 posts), poorly interlinked, not built on a keyword map
- No dedicated Injection Moulding page despite it being a core service
- No city/location landing pages (Mumbai, Haridwar, SIDCUL)
- No pricing/cost content — high-intent queries going unfulfilled
- No industry vertical pages

STRATEGY: Target "industrial coating + India" long-tail. Competitors (Mefron, Rustagi Polymers, Hydrographics India) rank with generic content — nobody systematically targets cost queries, city-specific B2B searches, and paint-defect educational content. 90-day sprint of ~24 blogs across 8 clusters recommended.
